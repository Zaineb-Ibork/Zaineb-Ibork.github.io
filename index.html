Research

Teaching Machines Where to Look, and How to Judge Visual Quality in 3D

A 3D Mesh is a graph before it is a picture. My work asks what that graph already knows: which regions a human eye will settle on, and how much distortion a surface can absorb before the damage becomes visible.

During my PhD, jointly supervised at the GREYC Laboratory in Caen and the SETIME Laboratory in Kenitra, I built no-reference quality metrics that judge a 3D Mesh without ever seeing the original, learning first from 2D projections, then from the geometry itself. At NII I work on saliency for textured 3D Meshes, combining graph-structural and topological descriptors with attention over local neighbourhoods.
